UTM link builder

Add campaign tracking parameters to a link so your analytics can tell where visits came from.

About this tool

UTM parameters are plain query-string tags that analytics tools read. Keep names consistent and lower case so reports do not split into near-duplicates, and never put personal data in them because URLs get logged and shared.

Free, runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded or tracked. More free tools.
