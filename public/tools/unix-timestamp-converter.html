Unix timestamp converter

Convert between Unix time and a readable date.

About this tool

Unix time counts seconds since 1 January 1970 UTC. Values above about 100 billion are treated as milliseconds. Dates are shown in UTC to avoid ambiguity; include a Z or an offset in ISO dates, otherwise the browser uses your local time zone.
