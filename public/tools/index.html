Free tools that run in your browser

Small, useful tools for freelancers, developers and small business sites. No sign-up, no tracking, nothing is uploaded.

  • WCAG color contrast checker

    Check text and background color contrast against WCAG 2.2 AA and AAA. Free, runs in your browser.

  • Freelance hourly rate calculator

    Work out the minimum hourly rate you need from your income goal, expenses, billable hours and working weeks.

  • Project quote calculator with risk buffer

    Build a fixed-price project quote from estimated hours, hourly rate, risk buffer and fixed costs.

  • Margin and markup calculator

    Calculate profit margin and markup from cost and price.

  • Late payment fee calculator

    Calculate a simple late fee and total due for an overdue invoice.

  • Local business JSON-LD generator

    Generate schema.org LocalBusiness structured data (JSON-LD) from a simple form. Free and private.

  • SRT subtitle time shifter

    Shift SRT subtitle timings earlier or later by a number of seconds. Runs in your browser; the file never leaves your computer.

  • Free invoice generator (print or save as PDF)

    Make a clean invoice in your browser and print it or save it as a PDF. No sign-up, nothing uploaded.

  • Website accessibility checklist (WCAG 2.2 AA)

    A plain-language WCAG 2.2 AA checklist for designers and developers. Ticks are saved in your browser.

  • Word and character counter with reading time

    Count words, characters, sentences and estimate reading time as you type. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

  • Aspect ratio and resolution calculator

    Find the aspect ratio of a width and height, or scale a resolution to a new width while keeping the ratio.

  • HEX, RGB and HSL color converter

    Convert a color between HEX, RGB and HSL and see a preview.

  • Text case converter

    Convert text to upper case, lower case, title case, sentence case, camelCase, snake_case or kebab-case.

  • UTM link builder

    Build a campaign URL with UTM parameters for source, medium, campaign, term and content.

  • Random password generator

    Generate strong random passwords in your browser using the Web Crypto API. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • HTML heading outline checker

    Paste HTML to see its heading outline and catch skipped levels, a missing h1 or multiple h1s. Runs in your browser.

  • Vague link text checker

    Paste HTML to find links with vague or empty text like click here or read more, which fail accessibility guidance.

  • Time zone converter

    Convert a date and time between time zones, including daylight saving changes.

  • Hourly to salary calculator

    Convert an hourly rate to a yearly salary and back, using your hours per week and weeks per year.

  • Percentage calculator

    Calculate X percent of a number, what percent one number is of another, and percentage change.

  • JSON formatter and validator

    Format, indent and validate JSON in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Base64 encoder and decoder

    Encode text to Base64 or decode Base64 back to text, with UTF-8 support, in your browser.

  • URL encoder and decoder

    Percent-encode or decode text for use in URLs and query strings.

  • Unix timestamp converter

    Convert a Unix timestamp (seconds or milliseconds) to a readable date and back.

  • Regex tester

    Test a JavaScript regular expression against sample text and see every match. Runs in your browser.
