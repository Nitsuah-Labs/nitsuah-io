Time zone converter

Pick a date and time and the zones to convert between.

About this tool

The entered time is read as local time in the From zone, then shown in the To zone using your browser's built-in time zone database, so daylight saving changes are handled for the date you choose. A shortlist of common zones is included. Times in the gap or overlap of a daylight saving change are ambiguous and may resolve either way.

Free, runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded or tracked. More free tools.
