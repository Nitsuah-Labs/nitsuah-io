Base64 encoder and decoder

Convert text to Base64 and back. UTF-8 characters are handled correctly.

About this tool

Base64 represents bytes using 64 printable characters. Text is converted to UTF-8 bytes first, so accented letters and emoji round-trip correctly. Base64 is an encoding, not encryption: anyone can decode it.

Free, runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded or tracked. More free tools.
