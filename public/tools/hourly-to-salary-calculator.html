Hourly to salary calculator

Convert between an hourly rate and a yearly amount.

About this tool

Yearly = hourly x hours per week x weeks per year. Enter a yearly amount to convert the other way; leave it at 0 to convert from hourly. Gross figures only, before tax and unpaid time off. For freelancers, see the hourly rate calculator, which accounts for expenses and non-billable time.

Free, runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded or tracked. More free tools.
