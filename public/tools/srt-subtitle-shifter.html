SRT subtitle time shifter

Fix subtitles that are out of sync by shifting every timestamp. Your file stays on your computer.

Choose a file to begin.

About this tool

SubRip (.srt) files store each subtitle with a start and end time. If a video was re-edited or the subtitles came from a different cut, every cue can be a fixed number of seconds off. This tool adds or subtracts that offset from all timestamps. Times never go below zero.

Free, runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded or tracked. More free tools.
