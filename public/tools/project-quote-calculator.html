Project quote calculator

Turn an hours estimate into a fixed price that includes a buffer for the unknowns.

About this tool

Quote = hours x rate x (1 + buffer) + fixed costs. Estimates are usually optimistic, so a 15 to 30 percent buffer is common for work with unknowns. Put the scope and what is out of scope in writing next to any fixed price.

Free, runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded or tracked. More free tools.
