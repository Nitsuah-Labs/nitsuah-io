Word and character counter

Paste or type text to count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs, with an estimated reading time.

About this tool

Words are runs of non-space characters. Reading time assumes about 238 words per minute, a commonly cited average for silent reading of non-fiction; speaking time is closer to 130 words per minute. Sentence counts are approximate because abbreviations also end with periods.

Free, runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded or tracked. More free tools.
